About

Hey! I'm Iyan. I recently graduated from the University of Pittsburgh with a degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering (3.7 GPA, Dean's Honors List, 2022–2025) and I'm now back home in Halifax, working as an Associate Data Engineer at IBM Canada.

I've been lucky to work on some genuinely interesting problems along the way: integrating depth cameras onto autonomous wheelchairs at the Human Engineering Research Laboratories, contributing to a distributed storage system at Microsoft, and building an ML-powered glove for real-time VR hand tracking as my senior project, and now building the ETL/ELT pipelines that feed enterprise analytics and machine learning at IBM. I still have a lot to learn, but I enjoy showing up and figuring things out.

I'm most at home working on systems problems, the kind where hardware and software have to meet each other halfway. Whether that's a real-time embedded control loop, a VHDL processor on an FPGA, or a cloud-native service running in Kubernetes, I like understanding why things work the way they do before trying to change them.

Outside of engineering I spend time reading about AI research, working through the occasional competitive programming problem, and slowly trying every coffee shop in Halifax. If you'd like to connect or just chat, feel free to reach out; I'm always happy to talk.
